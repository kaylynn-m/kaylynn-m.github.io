Mapping Flood Insurance Participation

Research
Using OpenFEMA data to map block group-level flood insurance participation in Coastal MS.

In this project, I used OpenFEMA data to map the distribution of National Flood Insurance Program (NFIP) coverage and identify areas with relatively low levels of participation.

The map below presents two measures of policy participation: the total number of effective residential NFIP policies and residential policies normalized by the number of housing units. Mapping both measures distinguishes places with large numbers of policies simply because they contain more households from places where flood insurance is more prevalent relative to the local housing stock.

Data & Methods: OpenFEMA NFIP Policies · U.S. Census/ACS housing data · R · sf · leaflet · rfema

All code used to extract OpenFEMA NFIP policy data and American Community Survey estimates, process the data, and create this map is available on GitHub here.
